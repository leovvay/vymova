You get four independent sources about the same lesson:
1. CANDIDATES — a first pass by a model that listened to the whole lesson. It over-reports and its timestamps can be off by up to a minute. Treat every candidate as a hypothesis.
2. CLIPS — every utterance of the student: clip id, time, the ASR text (may hide errors), the tutor's next reply if any (the tutor is NOT a native speaker; a reply that repeats a word is often a correction), and two BLIND narrow phonetic transcriptions made independently by two different models (A and B) that were not told what to look for.

Rules:
- A pattern is "confirmed" ONLY if for at least one example clip BOTH blind transcriptions A and B show the deviation. For every example give quotes: a short fragment copied VERBATIM (character by character, including stress marks and apostrophes) from transcription A of that clip and from transcription B of that clip.
- "possible" — only one blind transcription shows it (give the quote from that one, and an empty string for the other), or acoustic says likely and one transcription agrees.
